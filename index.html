🧠 What is AI Study Copilot?

AI Study Copilot is your personal study companion that makes learning smarter and faster. Simply upload your course materials (PDFs, notes, presentations), and the app creates an intelligent tutor that understands your content. You can ask questions and get answers with cited sources directly from your materials - no more searching through pages of notes!

The app automatically generates practice quizzes, analyzes your mistakes, and provides personalized study insights to help you improve. It's like having a private tutor available 24/7.

✨ Key Features

📚 Smart Tutor

Upload your materials and get instant answers with source citations. The AI understands your specific course content, not just general knowledge.

📝 Auto Quizzes

Automatically generated quizzes based on your materials. Test yourself anytime and track your progress over time.

🔍 Mistake Analysis

Get detailed analysis of your wrong answers. Understand why you made mistakes and learn the correct concepts.

📈 Study Insights

Personalized recommendations on what to focus on. See your strengths and weaknesses at a glance.

🔄 How It Works

  1. Upload your materials - PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint slides, or text files
  2. The AI processes your content - It creates a searchable knowledge base from your files
  3. Ask questions - Get answers with references to specific parts of your materials
  4. Take quizzes - Test your knowledge with auto-generated questions
  5. Review your progress - See insights and improve your study strategy
